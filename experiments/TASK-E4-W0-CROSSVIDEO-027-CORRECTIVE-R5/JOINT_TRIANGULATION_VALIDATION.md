|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| H0=I | 12.027° | 1.800 mm | 1.676 mm | 12 |
| H1=Ring | 3.858° | 0.928 mm | 0.925 mm | 12 |
| H2=Ring+RGB | **0.512°** | **0.156 mm** | 0.156 mm | 12 |
| H3=GT | 0° | 0.000 mm | 0.000 mm | 12 |
